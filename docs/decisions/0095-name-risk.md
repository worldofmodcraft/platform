# ADR-0095: The name is kept; renaming is kept cheap

- **Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Area:** Legal / Governance
- **Related:** ADR-0003, ADR-0060

## Decision
"World of Modcraft" deliberately echoes "World of Warcraft"; trademark risk (confusability with an aggressively defended mark) is distinct from the copyright line we designed around, and is hereby recorded with open eyes rather than left unsaid. Mitigations: the name is technically isolated (a constant; all occurrences generated — effectively free), descriptive marketing copy avoids "WoW"/Blizzard references, and the risk most plausibly materialises only with visibility — which sits behind Ludwig's announcement decision (ADR-0060). If a letter ever arrives, a rename is a boring week, not a catastrophe. This is not legal advice; it is risk bookkeeping. The decision is Ludwig's more than any other in this log.
