# ADR-0061: AI-generated assets are permitted under the originality rule

- **Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-09-02 · **Area:** Content / Legal
- **Related:** ADR-0004, ADR-0030, ADR-0046

## Decision
AI-generated assets (platform art, test-mod screenshots, mod assets) are allowed. The same rule as all assets applies: they must be **original** — AI re-creations of Blizzard style, motifs or specific assets ("prompt Stormwind") are forbidden on the same ground as upscales (ADR-0046). The uploader is responsible for holding rights to everything uploaded (a line added to content rules); the mod's licence field must cover all folder contents. The optional `ai_assisted` manifest flag (ADR-0030) covers voluntary labelling.
